# TALUQ PITCH KIT (Copy/Paste Ready)
April 2026

## 1) 1-Liner
Taluq is a zero-fee, offline-friendly, phone-number-first money operating system for Kenya that turns deposits and remittances into automatically settling, yield-generating lifestyle payments.

## 2) 30-Second Elevator Pitch
In Kenya, moving money is expensive and stressful: every bill, transfer, and utility payment can get hit with fees and friction, while idle cash earns nothing. Taluq fixes this by turning user deposits and global remittances into `yKES`—a yielding balance that auto-routes value to the user’s real-life obligations (power, water, rent, statutory) and enables 0.00 KES P2P transfers. The primary UX is a **Chat Wallet** where actions are commands (“balance”, “send”, “receive”, “deposit”). It’s built on Solana for sub-second settlement, uses Paystack for regulated fiat rails, and is designed for the “bundles economy” with offline-first PWA caching and USSD fallback.

## 3) Problem (What hurts)
- Fees: mobile money transfer fees punish daily life.
- Micro-leakage: idle balances lose value and do not earn.
- Remittances: families lose money to FX margins + transfer fees.
- UX: Web3 is too complex; seed phrases are non-starters for mainstream adoption.
- Trust: predatory apps harvest contacts; users fear privacy invasion.

## 4) Solution (What Taluq does)
- Phone-number identity: no seed phrases in the user experience.
- `yKES`: yielding balance designed for daily life.
- Omni-settlement: yield routes to monthly/weekly/on-demand obligations.
- 0.00 KES P2P: kill the fee model for internal transfers.
- Global Receive link: diaspora can send value instantly; recipient earns immediately.
- Merchant Till payments: seamless B2B settlement without merchants touching crypto.
- Chama vaults: group savings with multi-party controls and transparency.
- Offline-first: cache-first PWA for bundles; USSD fallback marker for coverage gaps.
- Privacy-first: zero phonebook access by design (no contacts harvesting).

## 5) Why Solana (Why now)
- Sub-second finality + low fees unlock micro-settlement economics.
- Token-2022 primitives support configurable token behaviors.
- Composability with stablecoin/yield primitives (USDY/USDC derivatives) enables local yield UX.

## 6) Why Paystack (Fiat bridge)
- Bank/card rails for deposits.
- Clean webhook flows for automated settlement.
- Compliance-friendly rails compared to ad-hoc cash systems.

## 7) What’s built (Demoable today)
- Chat Wallet UX: message-first wallet, quick actions, balance, send, receive, deposit.
- Webhook ingestion and forensic gating for deposits (min thresholds, signature verification).
- Offline-first service worker caching.
- Admin surface for monitoring.

## 8) Business Model (Simple, fundable)
- Yield spread: protocol earns yield, shares a safe portion with users.
- Interchange: virtual card usage can generate revenue on eligible rails.
- Enterprise rails: merchant settlement tooling can monetize through B2B pricing at scale.

## 10) FAQ (Fast answers)
### “Is this a bank?”
No. Taluq is a settlement + routing layer over regulated rails with cryptographic settlement guarantees. It is built to integrate with local compliance requirements over time.

### “How do you handle privacy?”
No phonebook access. No contacts scraping. Strict privacy posture and server-level restrictions where applicable.

### “Why will users switch?”
They don’t “switch” in one day. They adopt where Taluq is obviously better: remittances, recurring obligations, and fee-free internal transfers.

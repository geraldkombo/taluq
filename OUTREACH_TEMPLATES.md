## 2) Colosseum Hackathon Submission (Project Description)
**Name:** Taluq  
**Track:** Consumer / Payments  
**Tagline:** Zero-fee, offline-first money OS for Kenya with instant remittances and automatic settlement.

**Problem:** Daily money movement is burdened with fees, idle cash earns nothing, and remittances lose value to transfer fees/FX margins. Web3 UX is too complex for mainstream adoption.

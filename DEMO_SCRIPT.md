# TALUQ DEMO SCRIPT (2–3 Minutes)

## Setup (Before recording)
- Open Taluq on mobile (PWA “Add to Home Screen”)
- Ensure screen brightness is moderate (OLED black looks premium)
- Have the Chat Wallet ready (home screen)

## Script
### 0:00 — Hook
“In Kenya, moving money is expensive. Every payment has a fee. And when your money sits idle, it earns nothing. Taluq fixes that by turning deposits and remittances into a yielding balance that automatically settles real-life expenses.”

### 0:20 — Login (Phone-first)
“You don’t need seed phrases. This is phone-number-first identity. No crypto complexity.”

### 0:35 — Terminal (Vault)
“This is the Taluq Chat Wallet: a messaging-first wallet where money actions are commands. It’s designed for Kenyan bundles and low connectivity, with offline-first caching and a USSD fallback marker.”

### 0:55 — Deposit (Fiat on-ramp UX)
“Let’s top up. Deposit KES through bank/card rails. The goal is frictionless conversion into a yielding balance.”

### 1:15 — Yield and auto-settlement narrative
“Your money now generates yield continuously. The system routes that yield to life: utilities on demand, rent and statutory obligations monthly, and anything extra auto-compounds.”

### 1:35 — P2P send (fee disruption)
“Now, sending to another Kenyan phone number: type ‘send 07XX… 200’ or tap Send. 0.00 KES fee. Everyday transfers shouldn’t be burdened by avoidable charges.”

### 1:55 — Global Receive (Diaspora)
“Now type ‘receive’ or tap Receive. Diaspora sends via your global pay link. The recipient receives instantly and begins earning immediately. A remittance that grows instead of shrinking.”

### 2:15 — Close with ask
“We’re looking for partners and grant support to move from test rails to live rails, and to pilot with diaspora families, chamas, and merchants.”
